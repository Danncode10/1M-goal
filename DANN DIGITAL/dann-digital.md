## SEO Strategy for Filipino Market

### Domain & Hosting
- Use `.com` — trusted by Filipino businesses for tech/web services
- Host on Vercel + CDN — fast load times critical given Philippine internet speeds
- Point custom domain immediately after purchase — never use `.vercel.app` for live sites

---

### On-Page SEO (Content Strategy)
- Write in Filipino-friendly English — mix in natural Tagalog/Taglish phrases
- Target local intent keywords:
  - *"website for small business Philippines"*
  - *"affordable business website Philippines"*
  - *"web design para sa negosyo"*
  - *"website para sa Filipino businesses"*
  - *"website design Baguio"*
  - *"web designer Baguio City"*
- Each service must have its own dedicated page (not all crammed into one page)
- Maintain a blog with content Filipino business owners actually search for:
  - *"Bakit kailangan ng website ang iyong negosyo"*
  - *"How to get more customers online Philippines"*
  - *"Magkano ang website para sa maliit na negosyo"*

---

### Local SEO
- Create a **Google Business Profile** for DannDigital (even as a service-area business)
- List Baguio City as primary location
- Get listed on Filipino business directories:
  - Yello.ph
  - Pinoy Listings
  - BusinessList.ph

---

### Trust Signals (Critical for PH Market)
- Filipino businesses are relationship-driven — trust comes before payment
- Show your real face on the About page (photo + personal story — OJT to freelancer narrative)
- Display client logos and testimonials with real names and positions
- Add a **Facebook Messenger chat widget** — Filipinos use Messenger for business inquiries
- Show **GCash/Maya payment options** — signals you're a legitimate local business
- Collect and display Google reviews from day one
- Show specific numbers: projects completed, clients served, years of experience

---

### Backlinks
- Guest post on Filipino tech/business blogs
- Get featured in PH communities:
  - Filipino Freelancers Facebook group
  - Startup PH Facebook groups
  - Kalibrr community
- Contribute to Reddit: r/phclassifieds, r/Philippines business discussions
- Get listed on Clutch.co (same platform Carl Ocab uses — free to list)

---

### Technical SEO (Non-negotiables)
- Load time under 3 seconds on mobile — test with Google Lighthouse
- Mobile-first design — majority of PH users browse on phones
- HTTPS — handled automatically by Vercel
- Proper meta title, meta description, and OG image per page
- Sitemap + robots.txt submitted to Google Search Console

---

### Day-One Setup (Before Anything Else)
- **Google Search Console** — submit sitemap immediately at launch
- **Google Analytics** — track from day one, not after the fact
- **Google Business Profile** — set up even before the site is fully built

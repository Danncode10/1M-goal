## 📌 Tips Learned

1. **Skills drive the algorithm** — Upwork recommends jobs based on your skill tags, choose carefully
2. **n8n is a low-competition niche** — few Upwork freelancers list it, higher chance of ranking
3. **International client experience (ChrisAutoshine)** builds instant trust on the profile
4. **Don't add skills you can't deliver yet** — a bad first review kills the profile early
5. **Google Apps Script** is a good niche add — low competition, JS-based so easy to learn fast
6. **Title format:** Use `|` separators, lead with broadest role, end with niche differentiator

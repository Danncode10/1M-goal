# Upwork Connects & Bidding Strategy

A reference guide on how Connects work and how to spend them wisely.

---

## How to Make Money on Upwork

**1. Pick a niche and own it**
Don't be a generalist. Strongest niches based on my stack:
- Google Apps Script / Sheets automation
- Next.js + Supabase full-stack apps
- AI integrations (OpenAI API)
- React Native mobile apps

**2. Start with small, fast jobs**
Small scope = fast delivery = quick 5-star review. Reviews are everything early on.

**3. Write targeted proposals**
Don't use templates. Read the job post, mention the specific problem, show a relevant past project. 3–4 sentences max.

**4. Price strategically at first**
Start at $15–$25/hr to win first 3–5 jobs. Goal early is JSS (Job Success Score), not rate.

**The Formula:**
> Small job → Fast delivery → 5-star review → Raise rate → Repeat

---

## How Connects Work

- Each proposal costs **6–16 Connects** depending on the job
- Connects cost roughly **$0.15 each** (sold in bundles)
- New accounts get **10 free Connects** to start
- **100 Connects = $15.00**
- Connects expire **1 year from purchase**

---

## How Many Clients Can $15 Get You?

| Job Type | Connects/Proposal | Proposals from 100 Connects |
|---|---|---|
| Small jobs | 6 | ~16 proposals |
| Mid-size jobs | 12 | ~8 proposals |
| Large/featured jobs | 16 | ~6 proposals |

**Realistic outcome for a new freelancer:**
- Upwork's average proposal-to-hire rate for new freelancers is roughly **1 in 10–15**
- With 100 Connects: **1 client optimistically, possibly 0**

---

## Do You Lose Connects If Rejected?

**Yes.** Connects are spent the moment you submit a proposal — regardless of outcome:
- Client hires you → Connects gone
- Client rejects you → Connects gone
- Client never responds → Connects gone
- Client ghosts you → Connects gone

**The only way to get Connects back:**
- Client closes the job **without hiring anyone** → Upwork refunds your Connects
- Job is **removed/flagged as fraudulent** by Upwork → refunded

---

## How to Not Waste Connects

1. **Never copy-paste generic proposals** — waste of Connects
2. **Skip jobs with 50+ proposals** already — too competitive
3. **Avoid $0 spent clients** — likely never hire
4. **Only bid on jobs posted within 24 hours** — less competition
5. **Target jobs with 20–50 proposals** and a verified payment client
6. **Read the full job post** and reference specific details in your proposal

---

## Good Job Signals to Look For

- Payment method verified
- Client has $1K+ spent
- 50%+ hire rate
- Posted within 24 hours
- 20–50 proposals (not 50+)
- Small scope / less than 1 week

---

## The Hard Truth for New Freelancers

Your first $15 might get you **0 clients**. That's normal.

The goal of the first $15 isn't necessarily to land a client — it's to **learn what kind of proposals get responses.**

**Don't buy Connects until:**
- Profile is 100% complete
- Portfolio projects are added
- Hourly rate is set ($15–$20 to start)
- You have a strong proposal template ready

A weak profile wastes Connects. A strong profile can convert 1 in 5 proposals.
